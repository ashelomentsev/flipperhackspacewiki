# Getting inducted

Three machines in the workshop and the reflow oven are locked until you are signed off. The
[induction matrix](./induction-matrix.md) says which.

## Booking one

:::info[Needs a real answer — this is the membership bottleneck]
There is no booking process yet. Until there is, a new member cannot use the laser, the CNC, the
spray booth or the reflow oven at all, however keen they are.

What needs deciding:

- **Who can deliver each induction.** Maintainer only, or any signed-off member?
- **How it is requested** — a channel in Telegram, a bot command, a sign-up sheet.
- **When they run.** Scheduled slots beat on-demand: four people at once is the same hour as
  one, and new members mostly arrive in batches.
- **How long each takes**, so people can plan an evening.

Until then: ask in the members' Telegram and someone will sort it out informally.
:::

## What an induction is

Not a lecture and not a test. Someone who knows the machine shows you the machine, you use it
under their eye, and they sign you off when you are safe on it rather than good at it.

Expect:

1. **The hazards, specific to this machine** — not generic safety talk.
2. **The before-you-start checklist** from the tool page, done for real.
3. **A practical** — you run something small start to finish.
4. **The failure cases.** What it looks like going wrong, what you do, where the stop is.
5. **The sign-off**, or a "come back when you have watched someone else do it twice".

Being told to come back is a normal outcome, not a failure. The laser induction is the longest
in the building and people often need two goes.

## While you wait

Plenty to get on with:

- **The [electronics bench](../tools/electronics/index.md) needs no induction** — all 22 items,
  from day one. Read the [bench rules](../tools/electronics/index.md#bench-rules) and start.
- **Read the tool page before your induction.** You will get more out of the hour, and the
  known-quirks section is the part nobody tells you out loud.
- **Watch someone.** Ask. People like showing off a machine they are good at.
- **Get your design ready** so the practical is your actual project, not a test coupon.

## Keeping it

See the open questions on the [induction matrix](./induction-matrix.md) about expiry and
records. The sensible assumption until it is settled: if you have not touched a dangerous
machine in a long while, ask for a refresher rather than hoping muscle memory covers it.

## Inducting other people

Once you are experienced on a machine you may be asked to induct others. Two things to know
before you say yes:

- **You are signing your name to their competence.** If they hurt themselves next week, your
  sign-off is part of the story.
- **Teach the page, not your habits.** Everyone develops shortcuts; an induction is not where
  they get passed on. If the tool page is wrong, fix the page.
