## What "required" actually means

The machine is **locked**. Your [card](../start-here/how-to-get-in.md) logs you onto it, and if
you are not signed off it will not start. There is nothing to argue with and nothing to be
embarrassed about.

Using a locked-out tool — including one somebody else started for you — is one of the three
things that gets a membership reviewed on first offence.

## The two open items

:::info[Needs a real answer]
**1. The high-voltage induction is named but not defined.** "Anything above 50 V" appears
throughout this wiki as a two-person job, and the matrix says it needs an induction, but nobody
has written what that induction covers or who gives it. Given the space advertises sessions on
400 V battery packs, this is a real gap, not a formality.

**2. Who delivers each induction, and how long it takes.** Every tool page has this blank. Until
it is filled, a new member cannot book anything, which makes it the bottleneck on the whole
membership.
:::

## Keeping records

:::info[Needs a real answer]
Where sign-offs are recorded, who can see them, and how you check your own. Three things need
deciding:

- **The record itself** — the lockout system presumably holds it, but members should be able to
  see what they are signed off on without asking.
- **Expiry.** Does an induction lapse if you have not touched the machine in two years? Most
  spaces say yes for the dangerous machines.
- **Revocation.** Who can withdraw a sign-off, and on what grounds.
:::

Until this is answered, check with a space lead — and see
[Getting inducted](./getting-inducted.md).
