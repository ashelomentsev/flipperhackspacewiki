# Lone working

The space is open 24/7 and most of what happens here is fine to do alone at 3am. Four things
are not.

## What needs a second person

| | Why |
| --- | --- |
| [60 W CO₂ laser cutter](../tools/workshop/laser-cutter-co2.md) | Fire, and it moves fast |
| [Desktop CNC mill](../tools/workshop/cnc-mill.md) | Entanglement — the injury where you cannot free yourself |
| [Spray booth](../tools/workshop/spray-booth.md) and solvents | Vapour can overcome you with no warning |
| Anything above 50 V, including battery packs | Cardiac arrest, and burns that need an ambulance |

The common thread: these are the hazards where **being alone changes the outcome**, not just
the odds. A cut finger alone is a cut finger. A hand caught in a spindle alone is how people
die in workshops.

## What "a second person" means

**Awake, in the building, and knows you are using that machine.** Not asleep on the sofa, not
on another floor unaware, not "there was someone around earlier".

They do not need to be inducted on your machine. They need to be able to hit a stop, open a
door, and call 999 and say what you were doing.

Say it out loud before you start: *"I'm running the laser for the next hour."* That sentence is
the whole system.

## Before you start, alone or not

1. **Tap in**, so the board knows you are here. See
   [How to get in](../start-here/how-to-get-in.md).
2. **Tell someone when you expect to be out.** The members' Telegram counts.
3. **Phone charged and on you**, not across the room in a bag.
4. **Know where the first aid kit is** on your floor — and see the open question on
   [Layout](../the-space/layout.md#where-the-safety-equipment-is), because that location is not
   yet recorded.

## If you are alone and cannot get a second person

Don't do it. The space is open all night and the machine will still be there tomorrow.

The failure mode this rule exists to prevent is not recklessness, it is momentum: you have
driven across London, the file is sliced, the material is cut, and the only thing missing is
another person. Go home. That decision has never once been the wrong one in hindsight.

## Open questions

:::info[Needs a real answer]
- **Is there a check-in system**, or is the sign-in board it? Many spaces use a dead-man check:
  if you are still signed in at 6am, someone gets a message.
- **Who gets called** if a member is injured alone and the sign-in board is the only record.
  This needs a name and a number that works at 4am.
- **Does the space's insurance** place conditions on lone working? Insurers often do, and if
  there are conditions they override anything on this page.
:::
