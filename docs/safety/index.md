# Safety & inductions

The space has one safety principle and everything else follows from it: **the machines are more
dangerous than they look, and the person most at risk is the one who has used them twice.**

## The pages

- **[Induction matrix](./induction-matrix.md)** — the table. What needs a sign-off, and what each
  one covers.
- **[Getting inducted](./getting-inducted.md)** — how to book one, how long they take.
- **[PPE](./ppe.md)** — what to wear for what, and where it lives.
- **[Lone working](./lone-working.md)** — what you may not do alone, at any hour.

## The rules that apply everywhere

**No induction, no machine.** It is one of the three things that gets a membership reviewed on
first offence — see [House rules](../start-here/house-rules.md).

**Never defeat an interlock, a guard, a lockout or extraction.** If a machine will not start,
the answer is to find out why. The interlock is the only thing standing between the hazard and
the next person, and "it was being annoying" has never once been the real reason it tripped.

**Never left running unattended:** the [laser cutter](../tools/workshop/laser-cutter-co2.md),
the [CNC mill](../tools/workshop/cnc-mill.md), the
[spray booth](../tools/workshop/spray-booth.md) and the
[reflow oven](../tools/electronics/reflow-oven.md).

**Report it, always.** Broken tool, near miss, injury, a fire you put out in ten seconds. See
[Incidents & first aid](./incidents-and-first-aid.md). Nobody has ever been in trouble here for
reporting something.

**If you are unsure, stop.** Not "probably fine, I'll be careful". The space is open 24/7 and
the machine will still be there tomorrow when someone can show you.

## What the space does not have

Being straight about the limits, because they determine what is allowed:

- **No air-fed breathing apparatus and no health surveillance.** This is why two-pack and
  isocyanate products are banned in the [spray booth](../tools/workshop/spray-booth.md).
- **No mains-referenced test gear.** The [scope](../tools/electronics/oscilloscope.md) ground is
  tied to earth, which is why mains work is off the electronics bench entirely.
- **No anechoic chamber.** RF measurements are relative, not absolute — see the
  [shielded cage](../tools/rf/rf-shielded-cage.md).

:::warning[This section is a skeleton, not a safety management system]
These pages set out house practice and they are written to be useful at 3am. They are **not** a
substitute for the building's fire risk assessment, COSHH assessments, PAT testing schedule,
insurer's conditions, or the duties a UK employer and occupier has under the Health and Safety
at Work etc. Act 1974 and its regulations.

Several pages carry blanks that only a competent person can fill — flammable storage limits,
extraction figures, first aid provision, who is a named first aider. **Those blanks are not
wiki chores; they are commissioning tasks**, and the space should not take its first member
without them.
:::
