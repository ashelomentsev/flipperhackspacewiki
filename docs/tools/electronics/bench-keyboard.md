Wireless keyboard with a built-in touchpad — the touchpad works as a mouse. Switch on
at the top-right edge.

**Its tiny USB receiver must be plugged into the device you want to control.** That is the
whole of the troubleshooting: a keyboard that "doesn't work" is nearly always a receiver in a
different machine.

Runs on 2 × AA batteries. **Switch it off after use.**

## Don't

- **Don't take the receiver away with you.** It is the size of a fingernail and the keyboard is
  useless without it.

## Consumables

2 × AA batteries, bench shelf.

## If it breaks

Safe to try: find the receiver, fresh batteries, the power switch on the top-right
edge.
