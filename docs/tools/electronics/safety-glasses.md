Clear wrap-around glasses.

**Wear them for soldering, clipping leads and using hot air.** This is
[bench rule 1](./index.md#bench-rules).

The injury these prevent is not exotic: a clipped component lead leaves the
[cutters](./cutters-and-pliers.md) at eye height, and a bead of solder spits when flux boils.
Both happen at the bench, both happen fast, and neither is survivable by blinking.

Clean with a cloth and **leave them on the bench** — they are shared.

:::info[Needs a real answer]
How many pairs there are, and whether they are rated (EN 166) or dress-code glasses. It makes a
difference to what they are good for, and a pair of unrated clear glasses should not be
presented as eye protection.
:::

## Don't

- **Don't clean them with solvent.** It crazes polycarbonate and then you cannot see through
  them.
- **Don't take them home.**

## If it breaks

Scratched or crazed lenses are no longer eye protection — report them and they get
replaced.
