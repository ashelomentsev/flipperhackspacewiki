The rail holds Weller solder reels on each end and six colours of hook-up wire in
between.

## How to use

1. **Pull off what you need and cut it.** Don't take reels off the rail.
2. **Check the reel label for leaded or lead-free**, and set your iron to match — 330 °C leaded,
   350–370 °C lead-free. See [Main Soldering Station](./main-soldering-station.md).
3. **Red = power, black = ground** is the usual convention. Pick the other four colours on
   purpose and write down what you chose.

## Don't

- **Don't take a reel off the rail**, even to carry it two benches down.
- **Don't leave a wire end dangling** where the next person will pull a metre off by accident.

:::note[Leaded solder is on the rail and that's deliberate]
It is easier to learn on and better for rework. **Wash your hands after handling solder** —
[bench rule 6](./index.md#bench-rules) — keep it away from the café floor, and don't use it on
anything going out as a product. Both are on the rail; pick on purpose.
:::

## Consumables

Solder and hook-up wire, on the rail. **Post in the hackspace channel when a
reel runs low** — the rail only stays stocked because people do.
