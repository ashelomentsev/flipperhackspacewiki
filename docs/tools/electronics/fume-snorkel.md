A flexible arm with a funnel, ducted through the desk to the extraction system. It
pulls solder smoke away from your face.

Flux fume is a respiratory sensitiser. This is not a comfort feature.

## How to use

1. **Switch the extractor on before you solder.** Not once it starts to smell.
2. Bend the funnel to sit **10–20 cm from the joint, slightly behind it** — so the fume
   travels away from you rather than past your face.
3. **Leave it running a minute after you finish.**

## Don't

- **Don't point it at the ceiling** and call it on. If it isn't near the joint it is doing
  nothing.
- **Don't position it to pull across the [Microscope Camera](./microscope-camera.md) lens.**
  Flux fume fogs optics over time.

## If it breaks

Nothing is safe to try. If the arm isn't pulling — hold a tissue at the funnel to
check — the bench is not fit to solder at. Tag it and post in the hackspace channel.

A snorkel with weak extraction is worse than none, because people still solder under it.
