A 120 W station that heats a C210 tip in about two seconds. Lifting the iron from its
holder wakes it; putting it back sends it to sleep. This is the default iron for everyday
through-hole and SMD work — reach for the [Cordless Pen Iron](./cordless-pen-iron.md) only
for fine-pitch work and quick touch-ups.

## How to use

1. Switch on at the back. The screen shows the set temperature.
2. Pick a preset: **CH1**, **CH2** or **CH3**. Adjust with the **TEMP** arrows; **SET** saves
   it to that preset.
3. Typical temperatures: **330 °C** for leaded solder, **350–370 °C** for lead-free, lower for
   tiny SMD parts. Check the reel label on the [Solder & Wire Rail](./solder-and-wire-rail.md)
   and set the iron to match.
4. Lift the iron. Wipe the tip in the brass wool (blue cup), tin it with a little solder, then
   work.
5. Put the iron back in the holder between joints.
6. When done: tin the tip, return it to the holder, switch off.

## Changing tips

Spare tips stand in the holes on top. Grip the hot tip in the metal V-slot and pull the handle
straight out. Push the new tip in firmly. No tools needed.

## Don't

- **Don't file or scrape tips.** The working end is iron-plated; through the plating and the
  tip is scrap. A tip that will not take solder is almost always oxidised, not worn — re-tin it
  hot with plenty of solder and fresh flux.
- **Don't use the sponge dripping wet.** Damp only. A soaking sponge thermally shocks the tip.
- **Don't leave it on when you walk away.** The holder sleeps it; the bench does not.
- **Don't lay the iron on the bench.** Ever. It goes in the stand.

## Consumables

Solder and brass wool from the [Solder & Wire Rail](./solder-and-wire-rail.md)
and the bench shelf. Spare C210 tips stand in the holes on top of the station — post in the
hackspace channel when you fit the last one.

## If it breaks

Safe to try: swap the cartridge, re-seat the handle connector, power cycle.

A station that trips the bench RCD, smells of burning insulation, or has a damaged cord gets
unplugged and tagged. Don't open one.
