A heavy orange base with four bendable arms and crocodile clips.

## How to use

1. **Clip the board at its edge** — not across components.
2. Bend the arms to hold it steady. The base is heavy enough that you can push against it.
3. **Keep the clips away from components**, and away from anything you are about to heat.

A board that moves while you solder is the cause of most bad joints and most burns.

## Don't

- **Don't clip onto a connector, a crystal or a flex cable.** The jaws are stronger than they
  look.

## If it breaks

Arms that won't hold position, or clips that have lost their spring, are worth
reporting — a helping hand that droops is worse than none.
