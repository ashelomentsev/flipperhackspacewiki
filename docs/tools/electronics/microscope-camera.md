A 4K camera on a 0.7–4.5× zoom lens with an LED ring light, on a boom stand. It sends
its picture over HDMI to the [Microscope Screen](./microscope-screen.md) — **no computer
needed**.

Below 0603, or for any QFN, this is the difference between rework and guessing.

## How to use

1. Turn on the screen, then the camera. Its LED lights orange/green.
2. Turn on the ring light; set brightness with its side knob.
3. Put the board on the black base. Raise or lower the camera on the stand (big knurled knob)
   until roughly in focus.
4. Zoom with the ring marked **0.7–4.5**; fine-focus with the focus knob.
5. Settings menu — exposure, white balance, brightness: use the camera's buttons, or plug a
   mouse into the camera. **Leave settings as you found them.**
6. Photos and video save to the camera's SD card, if one is fitted.

## Don't

- **Don't touch the lens glass.**
- **Don't put a hot iron near the lens**, and don't blow
  [hot air](./hot-air-gun.md) at it. Both damage the optics.
- **Don't clean it with IPA and a lab wipe.** Lens tissue and a blower only; the coatings mark
  permanently.

## Known quirks

- **Straight-down ring light washes out the joint you are judging.** A shiny board reflects it
  back. Drop the brightness and come in oblique to see the fillet.
- **Flux fume fogs the lens over time.** Position the [Fume Snorkel](./fume-snorkel.md) to pull
  away from the objective, not past it.

## Consumables

Lens tissue and a blower bulb live with the camera. No solvents at the
microscope.

## If it breaks

Safe to try: check the screen's input (see [Microscope Screen](./microscope-screen.md)),
check the HDMI lead, power cycle both.

A camera that won't output, a ring light that flickers, or a boom that slips goes to the
maintainer.
