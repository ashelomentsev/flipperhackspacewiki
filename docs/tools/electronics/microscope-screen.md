Shows the [Microscope Camera](./microscope-camera.md) image.

If it says **No signal**, use the buttons under the bottom-right edge to switch input.

## Don't

- **Don't re-cable it to your laptop** and leave it that way. If you borrow the input, put it
  back on the camera before you go.

## If it breaks

Safe to try: input switch, re-seat the HDMI lead, power cycle.
