Flush cutters leave a flat cut on component leads. Pliers for gripping and bending.

## How to use

1. **Cut copper and component leads only.**
2. **Hold the offcut** so it doesn't fly. A clipped lead goes across the room at eye height,
   which is why [Safety Glasses](./safety-glasses.md) are bench rule 1.
3. Hang them back on the hooks.

## Don't

:::warning[Never cut steel with the flush cutters]
No steel wire, no guitar string, no nails, no screws, no hardened anything. One cut through
steel puts a notch in the blade and the cutter never cuts flush again — and the damage is
invisible until the next person gets a ragged lead.

Steel needs the workshop's side cutters, not these.
:::

## If it breaks

A notched or sprung cutter is finished for precision work. Report it — don't re-hook
it for the next person to discover.
