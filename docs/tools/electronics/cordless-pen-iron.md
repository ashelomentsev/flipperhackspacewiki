A 55 g battery iron for fine work. It takes the same **C210 tips** as the
[Main Soldering Station](./main-soldering-station.md), and charges magnetically in its dock.

## How to use

1. Press the power button on the pen. The screen shows battery or temperature.
2. In the dock it shows **SLP** — sleep, about 100 °C. Lift it and it heats to working
   temperature.
3. Adjust temperature with the side wheel/buttons; the screen shows the setting.
4. Clean on the sponge or brass pad in the dock base. Spare tips sit in the holes on top.
5. Always return it to the dock, so it sleeps and recharges.

A green light means it's ready.

## Don't

- **Don't take it off the bench.** It is the easiest thing in the lab to walk off with by
  accident.
- **Don't leave it lying outside the dock.** Out of the dock it neither sleeps nor charges, so
  the next person finds a flat iron.

## Consumables

C210 tips — shared with the main station, in the holes on top of each.

## If it breaks

Safe to try: swap the tip, seat it properly on the dock magnets, let it charge.

A pen that won't hold charge, gets hot at the grip, or has a swollen body goes straight to
tagged and reported — it is a lithium cell.
