## Floor rules

- **Everything here that can hurt you needs an induction.** Three of the four machines above are
  locked until you are signed off.
- **No food.**
- **No headphones while a machine is running.** The sound is how you know it is going wrong.
- **Eye protection** for the mill, and for anything that cuts, grinds or ejects. See
  [PPE](../../safety/ppe.md).
- **Swarf, sawdust and resin get cleaned up by whoever made them**, at the time. The vacuum is
  on this floor.
- **Never left running unattended:** the laser, the CNC and the spray booth. Not for five
  minutes, not while you fetch coffee. See [Layout](../../the-space/layout.md).
- **Two-person job out of hours:** the same three, plus anything above 50 V. See
  [Lone working](../../safety/lone-working.md).
