# How to get in

## Your card

One NFC card does three jobs:

1. Opens the street door and the internal doors you are cleared for.
2. Logs you onto the machines that need an induction, which is how the lockouts know who
   you are.
3. Identifies you on the sign-in board, so anyone can see who is in the building.

It is personal and non-transferable. Lending it to someone — including another member, and
very much including someone who is not a member — is the fastest route to losing it. The
reason is unglamorous: the card log is the only record of who was in the building when
something went wrong, and it stops being a record the moment cards float.

:::warning[Do not clone your own card]
You will be able to. Half the point of the space is learning how. Don't: the access log is
only worth anything while one credential means one person, and a cloned card is
indistinguishable from a stolen one at the door. Need a second credential for a legitimate
reason? Ask — there is a process. See [Responsible use](../flipper/responsible-use.md).
:::

## Hours

24/7 for members with a card. There is no closing time and nobody locks you in.

What changes out of hours is not access but what you may do alone — see
[Lone working](../safety/lone-working.md). The short version: the laser, the CNC, the spray
booth and anything above 50 V are two-person jobs, whatever the clock says.

Staffed hours, when there is someone around to ask, are on
[Opening hours](../the-space/opening-hours.md).

## Signing in

Tap in at the board by the lobby, even when the door is already open behind someone else.
It takes a second and it is what tells the fire marshal how many people to look for.

Tap out when you leave. If you are the last one out, the board tells you so — then follow
the closing list on [Layout](../the-space/layout.md).

## Guests

Members may bring one guest to a [bench session](../events/bench-sessions.md). Outside of
sessions, guests need to be arranged in advance in the members' Telegram.

A guest is your responsibility from the moment they come through the door: your induction
does not cover them, they do not touch a machine that needs one, and they leave when you do.

## When it does not work

| What happened | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Card does not open the street door | Message the members' Telegram — someone inside will let you in |
| Card works, machine says no | You are probably not inducted on it. Check the [induction matrix](../safety/induction-matrix.md) |
| Lost the card | Report it in Telegram **immediately** so it can be revoked. Replacement is in person, with ID |
| Door is wedged open | Unwedge it. No exceptions, no "I'm only outside for a minute" |
| Nobody is answering and you are locked out | The space will still be there tomorrow. Do not try to defeat the door |
