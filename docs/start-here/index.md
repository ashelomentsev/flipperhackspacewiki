# Start here

Flipper Hackspace is a hardware hackspace in Greenwich, London, run by the people who make
Flipper Zero. Ten thousand square feet over four floors: a workshop, an electronics lab,
resident studios, and the Flipper Devices team on the top floor.

It is a place to build things. That is the whole of it.

## What it is not

Being clear about this saves everyone a conversation:

- **Not a coworking space.** There are benches, not desks. If you want wifi and a chair,
  this is the wrong building.
- **Not bookable.** You cannot hire the space for an event. Members propose events; see
  [Propose an event](../events/propose-an-event.md).
- **No day passes.** Not now, not later. Access is membership only, by application.
- **Not a makerspace for watching.** Every event builds something. If you turn up to
  spectate you will be handed a soldering iron.

## Who it is for

People who have made something and want to make the next thing somewhere better equipped
than their kitchen table. Half-finished counts. A board that nearly worked counts. The
application asks what you have built, and that is genuinely the only filter.

## Read these four, in order

1. **This page** — what the place is.
2. **[How to join](./how-to-join.md)** — the application, what happens after, what it costs.
3. **[How to get in](./how-to-get-in.md)** — the door, your card, and the 3am rules.

About ten minutes. Then book your [inductions](../safety/getting-inducted.md), because the
laser, the CNC and the spray booth are locked until you have them.

## Then what

- Browse the **[tool register](../tools/index.md)** to see what is here and who looks after it.
- Read **[responsible use](../flipper/responsible-use.md)** if you are going to do anything
  with RF, NFC or access control. It is not optional reading and it is short.
- Put your build on a **[project page](../projects/index.md)**. The wall in the lobby is for
  finished things; the wiki is for things in progress.
