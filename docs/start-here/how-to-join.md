# How to join

Applications run through the members' Telegram bot:
**[@flipper_hackbot](https://t.me/flipper_hackbot)**. There is no form on the website and no
waiting list to sit on — the bot asks what you have built, and a human replies with a date.

## The three stages

### 01 — Apply

Show us something you made. A repo, a photo, a board that nearly worked, a thing that
caught fire. Half-finished is fine. Nothing is not.

We are not assessing quality. We are checking that you build things, because the whole
space depends on everyone in it being a builder rather than an audience.

### 02 — Come in once

One evening, invited. You build something small alongside two existing members. It takes
two or three hours.

This is the real step in both directions: it is how we meet you, and how you find out
whether the place suits you before paying for it. Some people come once and decide it is
not for them, which is a good outcome for everyone.

### 03 — Get the card

An NFC card that opens the door, your name on a locker, the members' Telegram, and every
[bench session](../events/bench-sessions.md) for the year.
