# House rules

Short on purpose. The long version is the rest of this wiki.

## The three that get a membership reviewed

Everything else on this page is a conversation. These three are not:

1. **Using a machine you are not inducted on.** Including "just for a second", including
   when someone else set it up for you.
2. **Defeating a safety interlock, a guard, a lockout or extraction.** If a machine will not
   start, the answer is to find out why.
3. **Lending your card, or letting someone in who should not be in.** See
   [How to get in](./how-to-get-in.md).

## Tidying

- **The bench you used goes back to empty.** Not tidy-ish. Empty, wiped, tools racked.
  Residents' own benches are the exception — that is what the tier is for.
- **Consumables you finished, you flag.** Last of the 0.6 mm solder, last sheet of 3 mm ply,
  last roll of filament: post it in Telegram. The stock only stays stocked because people do.
- **Swarf, sawdust, resin and solder spatter get cleaned up by whoever made them**, at the
  time, not later. The vacuum is in the workshop.
- **Last one out** follows the closing list on [Layout](../the-space/layout.md): extraction
  off, compressor bled, soldering irons off at the wall, lights, door.

## Borrowing

- **Hand tools stay in the building.** All of them. If you need a multimeter at home, buy a
  multimeter.
- **Instruments stay on their floor.** The scopes do not go to the workshop, the calipers do
  not go to the lab.
- **Nothing leaves without asking**, and "asking" means the maintainer, not whoever is
  nearest.
- **Put it back where its outline is**, not where there is space.

## Your stuff

- **A locker, and that is the allowance.** Member-tier storage is the locker. If it does not
  fit, it goes home.
- **Label work in progress** with your name and the date. Unlabelled things left on a shared
  bench get moved to the shelf, and after a month the shelf gets cleared.
- **The space is not insured for your equipment.** Bring your own scope if you like, but it
  is yours to lose.

## Living in a shared workshop

- **Noise.** Anything loud — mill, laser, compressor, angle grinder — gets a shout first.
  Headphones are fine; headphones while running a machine that needs you listening to it
  are not.
- **Food and drink.** Not on the electronics benches, not in the lab, never near the resin
  or the solvents. The café on the ground floor exists for this.
- **Smells.** Soldering flux is a shared experience. Resin printing, spray painting and
  anything with solvents happen in the booth with extraction running, with the door shut.
- **Music** in the workshop is whoever is in there. Headphones in the lab.
- **Photos.** Fine for your own build. Ask before you put someone else's face or someone
  else's unreleased project on the internet.

## Behaviour

The [code of conduct](../how-we-run/code-of-conduct.md) is the binding document and it is
worth reading properly. The summary is: the space is full of people who are better than you
at something and worse than you at something else, and everyone is here to build. Act like it.
