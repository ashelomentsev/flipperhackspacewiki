# Layout

Four storeys. The numbering on the stairwell is the numbering used everywhere in this wiki
and on the machine labels.

## 00 — Lobby & café

The sign-in board, the lockers, coffee, and the wall where finished projects go. Meetings
with people who are not members happen here, not upstairs.

Also on this floor: the e-waste bin, which is free to raid. Take anything from it. Put
anything into it that someone might want, and take genuinely dead things to the recycling
rather than making it someone else's sorting problem.

## 01 — Workshop

The loud, dusty floor. 3D printing, the [CO₂ laser cutter](../tools/workshop/laser-cutter-co2.md),
the [CNC mill](../tools/workshop/cnc-mill.md), the [spray booth](../tools/workshop/spray-booth.md),
hand tools, and a lot of extraction.

Everything on this floor that can hurt you needs an induction. No food, and no headphones
while a machine is running.

## 02 — Electronics lab

Twelve anti-static benches, the microscope row, the
[reflow oven](../tools/electronics/reflow-oven.md), and the
[RF-shielded test cage](../tools/rf/rf-shielded-cage.md).

ESD discipline applies: wrist strap on the bench stud before you handle bare boards. No food
or drink on this floor at all — not at the far end, not "just a bottle".

## 03 — Studios & Flipper HQ

Resident desks, and the Flipper Devices team on the same floor. Residents' benches are their
own and stay as they left them.

The team being here is a feature of the membership, not a support desk. Showing someone the
board that keeps browning out and asking why is exactly the intended use. Treating them as
your firmware helpdesk is not.

## Closing list — last one out

The sign-in board tells you when you are the last person in the building. Then:

1. **Extraction off** — workshop and spray booth.
2. **Compressor bled** and switched off.
3. **Soldering stations off at the wall**, not just at the iron.
4. **3D printers**: a running print may be left. Anything else gets powered down.
5. **Laser, CNC, reflow oven**: off and cold. Never left running unattended, at any hour.
6. **Benches clear**, bins not overflowing.
7. **Internal doors shut** — they are fire doors.
8. **Lights**, then the street door. Check it latched behind you.

:::warning[Never left running unattended]
The laser cutter, the CNC mill, the reflow oven and the spray booth. Not for five minutes,
not while you go downstairs for coffee. A laser cutter fire takes under a minute to get out
of hand and the machine is on a floor with a lot of dust and plywood.
:::
