# The space

**8 Soames Walk, London SE10 0AX.** Four storeys, about 10,000 sq ft, four minutes' walk
from North Greenwich on the Jubilee line.

## The four floors

| Floor | What is on it |
| --- | --- |
| **00** | Lobby & café — lockers, coffee, and the wall where finished projects go |
| **01** | Workshop — 3D printing, laser cutter, CNC, spray booth, and a lot of extraction |
| **02** | Electronics lab — twelve anti-static benches, microscope row, RF-shielded test cage |
| **03** | Studios & Flipper HQ — resident desks, and the Flipper Devices team on the same floor |
