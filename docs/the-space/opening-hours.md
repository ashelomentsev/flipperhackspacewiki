# Opening hours

## Open

**24/7, for any member with a card.** There is no closing time.

## Staffed

:::info[Needs a real answer]
When there is reliably someone around — a space lead, a maintainer, a first aider — needs
setting and then publishing here. This is the single most-asked question after "what does it
cost", and "24/7" is not an answer to it.
:::

Staffed hours matter for three things: getting an induction, getting a second person for a
two-person job, and getting help when a machine misbehaves.

## What changes out of hours

Access does not change. What you may do **alone** does.

These need a second competent person in the building, at any hour:

- CO₂ laser cutter
- CNC mill
- Spray booth and anything with solvents
- Anything above 50 V, including mains work and battery packs over 50 V
- Anything with a stored-energy hazard — large capacitor banks, charged packs

The full rule, including what counts as "competent" and the sign-in convention, is on
[Lone working](../safety/lone-working.md).

[Bench sessions](../events/bench-sessions.md) and other events run on their own schedule,
announced in the members' Telegram and on the landing page. Members may bring one guest to a
