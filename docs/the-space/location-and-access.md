# Location & access

**8 Soames Walk, London SE10 0AX.** [Open in Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=8+Soames+Walk+London+SE10+0AX)
· 51.4997, 0.0057

## Getting here

| From | Time |
| --- | --- |
| North Greenwich (Jubilee) | 4 min walk |
| Canary Wharf | 8 min |
| London Bridge | 14 min |
| Greenwich (DLR / National Rail) | _unmeasured — someone who walks it should time it_ |

Buses to North Greenwich: the station is a major interchange, so most routes that reach the
peninsula will do.

## By bike

:::info[Needs a real answer]
Whether there is bike parking, where, and whether bikes can come inside. Needs confirming
with the building before anyone relies on it.
:::

## By car

There is no member parking. The Greenwich Peninsula is inside the ULEZ and street parking
around Soames Walk is controlled. Public car parks exist near the O2, at O2-event prices.

For dropping off something heavy, see **Deliveries** below rather than planning to park.

## Deliveries and heavy things

:::info[Needs a real answer]
The loading arrangements — which entrance, whether there is a goods lift, floor loading
limits, and whether anyone can sign for a parcel when you are not in the building. This
matters more than it sounds: it determines whether a member can have a 40 kg machine
delivered. Needs an answer from whoever manages the building.
:::

Until that is answered: arrange any delivery in the members' Telegram first, and do not
have anything sent to the space without telling someone.

## Step-free access

:::info[Needs a real answer]
Lift access to floors 01–03, accessible WC, door widths, and whether the street entrance is
level. This should be a straight factual answer on a public page, not something an applicant
has to ask for — someone needs to walk the building and write it up.
:::

If you need to know before you visit, email
[hackspace@flipper.net](mailto:hackspace@flipper.net) and we will give you a real answer
rather than a guess.
