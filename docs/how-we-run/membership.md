## What membership obliges you to

Membership is not only a payment. By holding a card you are agreeing to:

1. **The [house rules](../start-here/house-rules.md)**, and the three that get a membership
   reviewed on first offence.
2. **No machine without its [induction](../safety/induction-matrix.md).**
3. **The [code of conduct](./code-of-conduct.md).**
4. **[Responsible use](../flipper/responsible-use.md)** — the RF, NFC and access-control policy.
5. **Reporting incidents and near misses**, including the ones that were your fault. See
   [Incidents & first aid](../safety/incidents-and-first-aid.md).
6. **Your card being yours alone.** See [How to get in](../start-here/how-to-get-in.md).
7. **Your guests' behaviour**, for as long as they are in the building.

## Your stuff

- **A locker is the allowance** at Member tier. If it does not fit, it goes home.
- **The space is not insured for your equipment.** Bring your own scope if you like; it is yours
  to lose. Consider your own contents cover.
- **Label work in progress.** Unlabelled things on a shared bench get moved to the shelf, and the
  shelf gets cleared monthly.
- **Residents' benches** are theirs for the term of the tier, not indefinitely.

## Withdrawal

Membership can be withdrawn. The grounds, in rough order of how fast they act:

| Grounds | Typically |
| --- | --- |
| Breach of [Responsible use](../flipper/responsible-use.md) | Immediate, and the space cooperates with any investigation |
| Serious breach of the [code of conduct](./code-of-conduct.md) | Immediate |
| Using a machine you are not inducted on | Reviewed on first offence |
| Defeating an interlock, guard or extraction | Reviewed on first offence |
| Lending your card, or letting in someone who should not be in | Reviewed on first offence |
| Hiding a fault that then endangered someone | Reviewed |
| Non-payment | Per the terms |
| Repeatedly leaving the space a mess after being asked | Eventually, and nobody enjoys it |

:::info[Needs a real answer]
**There is no appeal process written down.** A space that can withdraw a membership immediately
should say who hears a challenge and on what basis — not for legalism, but because it is the
difference between a rule and a mood. Needs deciding by Flipper Technologies Ltd.
:::

## The honest note

Membership is by application, at a company's discretion, and can be withdrawn. See
[How we run](./index.md) — this is a company-run space, not a co-operative, and the terms
reflect that.
