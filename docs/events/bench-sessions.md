# Bench sessions

The flagship series. **Six a year**, included in Member and Resident tiers, plus one guest each.

The format: someone who has actually done the thing brings the thing, and everyone in the room
builds a piece of it. Not a talk about a subject — an evening ending with twelve people holding
something that works.

## The series

From the landing page. **Dates, hosts and running order are not fixed yet.**

| Session | You leave with |
| --- | --- |
| How to put a 400 V pack in a 1972 Land Rover | A working BMS board you flashed and bench-tested |
| Walking robot — how hard can it be? | A servo-driven leg linkage, cut and assembled here |

| A satellite engineer, using the dish on our roof | An SDR ground station and a decoded pass |
| A founder who shipped 100,000 units of one small device | A bring-up checklist and your board on the scope |

## Two sessions worth noting

**The 400 V pack.** High-voltage work is a two-person job and needs an induction that
[does not exist yet](../safety/induction-matrix.md#the-two-open-items). A session built around a
400 V pack needs that sorted first, with a competent person running it — not as paperwork, but
because a room of twelve people around a battery pack is exactly the situation the rule is for.

**The satellite ground station.** Receive-only, which keeps it clear of
[Responsible use](../flipper/responsible-use.md) entirely. Worth saying out loud at the session,
because an SDR evening is where someone's instinct to transmit shows up.

## What to bring

:::info[Needs a real answer]

Generally: your [Flipper](../flipper/index.md) if the session uses one, a laptop, and your own
safety glasses if you have a pair you like. The space provides the
[bench kit](../tools/electronics/index.md).

## The rules at a session

- **[Bench rules](../tools/electronics/index.md#bench-rules) apply**, and more so with twelve
  people and one fume snorkel each.
- **No inducted machine without the induction**, session or not. Hosts cannot wave this.
- **No live demonstrations against third-party systems.** See the event rules in
  [Responsible use](../flipper/responsible-use.md). This is the tightest-held rule in the
  building and any member can stop a session that drifts.
- **Your guest is yours.** All evening.
- **Tidy up.** Twelve people leaving a bench each is how a lab becomes unusable by Tuesday.
