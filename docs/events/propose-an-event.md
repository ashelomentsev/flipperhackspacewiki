# Propose an event

Members propose events. The space does not take bookings and cannot be hired — see
[Events](./index.md).

## The bar

**Everyone leaves having built something.** That is the whole test.

It is not expertise. The best sessions are often run by someone one step ahead of the room, who
remembers which part was confusing. What it is not is a talk with slides, and it is not a demo
people watch.

Turning an idea into a session that clears the bar means answering: *what is in their hands at
the end?*

- ❌ "How brushless motor control works"
- ✅ "Everyone leaves with a motor spinning off a dev board they flashed"
- ❌ "An introduction to RF"
- ✅ "Everyone decodes a weather station transmission they captured themselves"

## What to put in the proposal

Post it in the members' Telegram. Six things:

1. **What people leave with.** One sentence, concrete.
2. **How many people**, and the bench space it needs. Twelve benches is the hard limit on the
   lab floor.
3. **What they bring, what the space provides, what you need bought.** With a number if money is
   involved.
4. **Which [tools](../tools/index.md) it uses**, and **whether any need an
   [induction](../safety/induction-matrix.md)**. If they do, say how that is handled — you cannot
   wave it for an evening.
5. **How long**, and the rough shape of the evening.
6. **Non-members attending?** Say so up front; it changes who needs to approve it.

## What needs approval, and from whom

| Your event | Needs |
| --- | --- |
| A [recurring night](./recurring-nights.md), members only, no spend, no inducted machines | Nothing. Post it and run it |
| Uses an inducted machine | The tool's maintainer, on how competence is handled |
| Spends the space's money | A space lead |
| Non-members in the building | A space lead — insurance and access |
| Press, filming, or anything public-facing | [hackspace@flipper.net](mailto:hackspace@flipper.net), not Telegram |
| High voltage, solvents, or anything in the [lone-working](../safety/lone-working.md) list | A space lead **and** a competent person running it |
| A slot in the six-a-year [bench sessions](./bench-sessions.md) | A space lead |

See [How decisions get made](../how-we-run/how-decisions-get-made.md) for how these calls work
generally.

## The rules you cannot waive

- **No inducted machine without the induction.** Not for one evening, not with you watching.
- **No live demonstrations against third-party systems.** The event rules in
  [Responsible use](../flipper/responsible-use.md), and they are tighter for events than for
  individuals on purpose: a room of twenty contains someone who will try it on the way home.
- **Nothing on the policy's prohibited list gets taught**, including as a "this is what's
  possible" aside.
- **The [code of conduct](../how-we-run/code-of-conduct.md) applies**, and as host you are the
  first person people will come to.

## Running it well

- **Build it yourself first, start to finish, on the space's kit.** Every session that went badly
  skipped this.
- **Over-order consumables.** Something always gets destroyed.
- **Plan for the slowest person.** Have a parked, half-built version for whoever falls behind.
- **Get a second pair of hands.** Twelve people and one host means eleven people waiting.
- **Finish on time**, then let people stay. The space is open 24/7 and the good conversations
  happen after.
- **Write it up.** What worked, what you would change, what to order more of. It makes the next
  one easier — for you or whoever runs it next.
